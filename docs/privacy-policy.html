Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

Music Buddy ("we", "our", or "the application") is an open-source, educational mobile application designed to help users learn music theory fundamentals. We take privacy seriously and believe educational apps should respect user privacy completely.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Music Buddy does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: There are no third-party telemetry, tracking SDKs, or analytics services embedded in the application.
  • No Location Data: The application never requests or accesses GPS or device location information.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not track unique hardware IDs (IMEI, MAC address, advertising IDs).

3. Device Permissions & Data Storage

Music Buddy operates locally on your device:

  • Local Preferences: User settings (such as music mute preferences) are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's standard SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.
  • External Links: The application contains links to external educational resources (such as MusicTheory.net and YouTube). When you click an external link, you are directed to that third-party website, which is governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Because Music Buddy is an educational music tool, it is suitable for learners of all ages, including children under 13. In full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR, we do not collect any personal information from any user, including children.

5. Open Source Code

Music Buddy is open-source under the GNU General Public License v3.0. You can inspect the complete source code at any time on our public GitHub repository: https://github.com/tstytrd/music-buddy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/tstytrd/music-buddy/issues.
